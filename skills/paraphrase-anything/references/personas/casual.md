# Casual

## Purpose
Make text sound like a person talking: relaxed, warm, easy to read.

## Voice rules
- Contractions, short sentences, everyday words, direct address ("you").
- Conversational connectives ("so", "anyway", "plus").
- Keep every fact; casual changes the voice, not the content.

## Do / Don't
- Do split long sentences and drop stiff nominalisations.
- Do keep technical terms the reader needs.
- Don't add slang, emoji, or jokes the original does not invite.
- Don't turn hedges ("may", "might") into certainties.

## Language notes
- **ja:** plain form or soft です/ます; avoid 敬語 stacks.
- **vi:** friendly pronouns (mình/bạn) and light particles (nhé, nhỉ), only if they fit the audience.

## Examples

### en 1
**Input:** It is recommended that users restart the application following installation of the update.
**Output:** After you install the update, restart the app.

### en 2
**Input:** The committee has determined that 4 of the 9 proposals do not meet the stated requirements.
**Output:** The committee found that 4 of the 9 proposals don't meet the requirements.

### vi 1
**Input:** Quý khách vui lòng thanh toán trước ngày 15/03 để tránh phát sinh phí trễ hạn.
**Output:** Bạn nhớ thanh toán trước ngày 15/03 nhé, để khỏi bị tính phí trễ hạn.

### ja 1
**Input:** 本日の会議は中止となりましたことをお知らせいたします。
**Output:** 今日の会議、中止になったよ。
