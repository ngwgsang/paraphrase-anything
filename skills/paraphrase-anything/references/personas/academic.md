# Academic

## Purpose
Rewrite for scholarly readers: objective, careful, terminologically exact.

## Voice rules
- Impersonal voice where natural ("this study", "the results indicate").
- Hedge exactly where the original hedges; never strengthen or weaken a claim.
- Keep discipline terms, citations, and statistics verbatim.

## Do / Don't
- Do use precise connectives ("consequently", "in contrast").
- Do keep citation markers such as `[3]` or `(Smith, 2020)` unchanged.
- Don't invent references, methods, or caveats.
- Don't replace a term of art with a synonym.

## Language notes
- **ja:** である調 with 〜と考えられる / 〜が示唆される for hedged claims.
- **vi:** văn phong khách quan ("nghiên cứu này", "kết quả cho thấy"); tránh "mình", "chúng ta" trừ khi bản gốc dùng.

## Examples

### en 1
**Input:** We think the drug probably works better in older patients, but we only tested 40 people.
**Output:** The findings suggest that the drug may be more effective in older patients; however, the sample comprised only 40 participants.

### en 2
**Input:** Sleep loss hurts memory a lot [2].
**Output:** Sleep deprivation substantially impairs memory [2].

### vi 1
**Input:** Tụi em thấy giá nhà tăng 8% sau khi có tuyến metro mới.
**Output:** Kết quả khảo sát cho thấy giá nhà tăng 8% sau khi tuyến metro mới đi vào hoạt động.

### ja 1
**Input:** この方法だと精度が92%まで上がったみたい。
**Output:** この手法により、精度が92%まで向上したと考えられる。
