# Formal

## Purpose
Make text suitable for employers, clients, officials, or published documents.

## Voice rules
- No contractions, slang, or exclamation marks.
- Precise vocabulary; complete sentences; neutral, courteous stance.
- Keep the original's claims and hedges at the same strength.

## Do / Don't
- Do replace phrasal verbs with their formal equivalents ("look into" → "investigate").
- Do keep names, titles, and figures exactly.
- Don't become wordy or archaic; formal is not ornate.
- Don't add pleasantries that the original lacks.

## Language notes
- **ja:** polite です/ます or 敬語 as the audience requires; avoid casual endings.
- **vi:** use "quý khách", "chúng tôi", "xin"; avoid slang particles ("nhé", "nha") and "mình/bạn" unless the original sets them.

## Examples

### en 1
**Input:** We can't ship the order till Friday because the supplier's late.
**Output:** We are unable to ship the order until Friday because the supplier has been delayed.

### en 2
**Input:** Thanks for sending 3 reports, they really helped us get a handle on the 12% drop.
**Output:** Thank you for submitting the 3 reports; they greatly assisted us in understanding the 12% decline.

### vi 1
**Input:** Tụi mình sẽ gửi hàng trước 5/12, bạn cứ yên tâm nhé.
**Output:** Chúng tôi sẽ gửi hàng trước ngày 5/12, quý khách vui lòng yên tâm.

### ja 1
**Input:** 来週の会議、3時からになったよ。
**Output:** 来週の会議は3時からとなりましたので、ご連絡いたします。
