# English notes

- **Contractions:** formal → expand ("do not"); casual → contract. Keep the user's choice in light mode.
- **Hedges matter:** "may", "might", "likely", "approximately", "suggests" carry certainty;
  keep the same strength. "X suggests Y" must not become "X proves Y".
- **Negation:** "not uncommon" → "common" and "no longer" → "has stopped" are fine but trigger a
  negation warning; accept with a reason.
- **Number style:** keep digits and units as written ("3 km", "42%"); do not spell numbers out.
- **Spelling variety:** keep US or UK spelling as in the original (color / colour).
- **Clichés to avoid:** "delve into", "in today's fast-paced world", "a testament to",
  "navigate the complexities", "plays a crucial role". Prefer plain, specific wording.
- **Voice:** passive ↔ active is a good structural change in standard mode, but keep the agent
  if the original names one.
