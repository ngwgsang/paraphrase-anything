# Chinese (Simplified) notes

- **Table headers:** 原文 · 改写后 · 修改类型
- **Script:** keep Simplified characters; never switch to Traditional (or mix the two) unless asked.
- **Register:** 书面语 (written, formal) vs. 口语 (spoken, casual). Formal text avoids 啊/吧/呢 and
  slang; keep one register throughout.
- **Address:** keep 您 vs. 你 as in the original; 您 in a formal text must not become 你.
- **Length unit:** `pe metrics` counts characters for Chinese, not words.
- **Hedges:** 「可能」「也许」「似乎」「大概」「估计」 carry certainty; keep the same strength.
  「可能会增加」 must not become 「会增加」.
- **Negation:** 「不」「没（有）」「未」「无」「别」. Rewriting 「并非不重要」 as 「很重要」 is fine
  but will raise a warning; accept with a reason. Words that only contain 不 (e.g. 不断, 不仅)
  are counted too, so a warning may need an accept when such a word is replaced.
- **Proper nouns:** `pe check` cannot detect them for Chinese (reported as `unsupported`);
  check names, places, and organisations yourself.
- **Numbers:** keep Arabic vs. Chinese numerals as written (30人 / ３０人 are equal; 三十人 is not
  recognised as 30). Keep 万/亿 units exactly: 3万 must not become 30,000.
- **Punctuation:** use full-width Chinese punctuation (，。！？「」) in Chinese prose.
- **Clichés to avoid:** 「在当今社会」「发挥着重要作用」「众所周知」「随着时代的发展」.
